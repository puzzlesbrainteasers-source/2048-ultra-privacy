Welcome to 2048 Ultra. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it.

2048 Ultra is a puzzle game. The App does not require you to create an account or provide your name, phone number, email address, contacts, or other personal information directly to us.

  • IP address
  • Country or approximate location

  • Information about ad impressions and interactions
  • Information used to detect and prevent invalid or fraudulent advertising activity

Google's advertising services may use this information to provide, measure, personalize, and protect advertising services, subject to applicable settings, consent requirements, and Google's policies.

We do not use the App to directly collect your name, phone number, email address, contacts, microphone recordings, camera data, or precise GPS location.

The App uses Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service. Google may process information according to its own privacy policies and applicable terms.

For more information, see Google Privacy Policy.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

2048 Ultra does not maintain a user account or a developer-controlled personal profile for you. There is therefore no user account that you need to delete from our systems.

Information processed by third-party advertising providers is subject to those providers' applicable retention, deletion, and privacy policies.

7. Children's Privacy

2048 Ultra is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

We do not operate a server that stores personal user accounts for 2048 Ultra. Information transmitted to third-party services such as Google AdMob is handled according to those services' security practices and applicable policies.
